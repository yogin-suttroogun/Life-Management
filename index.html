Week plan · 2 jobs

My Training Week

8h30 of sleep and 2 hours of training every day, around 7.5h of software work (plus 30-min lunch and 15-min tea) and badminton coaching.

Week at a glance

Rules that make it work

Bedtime is fixed

Every wake time is set from the bedtime the night before. If one slips, cut free time, not sleep.

Prep dinners ahead

Tue and Thu need dinner on the table at 8:20. Cook 4–5 dinners on Saturday or Sunday.

Light evenings

Tue/Thu evening sessions are mobility, yoga, core and stretching only, so they help sleep.

Sunday is recovery

After 5.5 hours of coaching, make the 2 hours easy: a long walk, an easy swim, stretching.

Take the rides

A ride home after coaching saves 20 minutes. Use it for wind-down, not more screen time.

Office days: Wednesday only

Mon, Tue, Thu and Fri can't fit a 3-hour round trip before coaching. On Wednesday sleep stays at 8h30, training drops to 45 min, and Saturday's session runs an hour longer.

Lunch and tea are inside every work window. Work totals 7h30 each weekday.
